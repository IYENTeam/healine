## Decision and scope

Reviewed on 2026-09-28. Healine separates overnight recovery, sleep characteristics,
daytime activity and observed heart rate. It compares a person's observations
under similar recorded activity and time conditions. It does not estimate a
psychological stress probability or infer causation from Calendar events.

The sources below inform that separation and the data contract. They do not
validate the numeric matching tolerances or display boundary implemented here.
Those remain explicit engineering choices, documented in the
[collector README](../../integrations/polar-calendar/README.md).

## Research considered

| Primary source | Relevant evidence and limits | Implementation consequence |
|---|---|---|
| [Kargarandehkordi et al., npj Mental Health Research, May 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44184-026-00210-9) | A four-week observational study of 20 adults with self-reported hypertension compared individual and pooled models using wearable signals, momentary stress ratings and blood pressure. Its performance is not transferable to an unlabeled single-person Polar feed. | Use personal history; do not borrow a published model's accuracy or label heart-rate deviations as psychological stress. |
| [Presby et al., JMIR, 2025](https://www.jmir.org/2025/1/e64955/) | A large longitudinal study relates wearable physiology and monthly mental-health surveys, including within-person variation. These associations are not moment-to-moment causal detection. The abstract was accessible; the publisher's full text was blocked during this review. | Show dated overnight trends and their reference population, separate from daytime observations. |
| [Unobtrusive stress detection using wearables, Frontiers in Computer Science, 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computer-science/articles/10.3389/fcomp.2025.1575404/pdf) | A university-setting study examines real-world wearable monitoring with noisy and incomplete observations. Group-level results cannot validate an individual's stress label. | Retain missingness and activity context; expose the number of comparable dates and windows. |
| [Nazaret et al., npj Digital Medicine, 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-023-00926-4) | A personalized model describes heart-rate response to exercise and environmental conditions in a large workout dataset. It is an exercise/fitness model, not a psychological stress detector. | Match recent activity as well as current activity; avoid treating activity-related recovery as unexplained tension. |
| [Detecting prolonged stress in real life, JMIR, 2023](https://www.jmir.org/2023/1/e39995) | The naturalistic study combines physiological recording with ecological momentary assessments. Its design distinguishes exposure, appraisal and physiological response. The accessible indexed abstract informed this review. | Calendar plans and physiological changes do not replace subjective labels. Optional notes remain useful without becoming mandatory input. |
| [Brown et al., arXiv, August 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03251) | A small seven-participant pilot combines event annotations and wearable data. This is a preprint and exploratory feasibility evidence, not proof of diagnostic or causal accuracy. | Keep source measurements and optional context available for later reflection; do not train or validate a stress model on its own generated labels. |

## Vendor API and SDK checks

- [Polar AccessLink Dynamic API v4](https://www.polar.com/polar-api-v4/) and its
  [Swagger contract](https://www.polar.com/polar-api-v4/swagger.yaml) define the
  existing cloud pipeline. Activity and featured sleep requests remain bounded
  to a day. Recovery fields retain their units and source names. Optional zero
  interval/HRV sentinels are missing values; zero signed ANS status is valid.
- [Nightly Recharge](https://support.polar.com/en/nightly-recharge-recovery-measurement)
  separates autonomic recovery from sleep charge. ANS ratings are ordered levels,
  not stress percentages. ANS and its physiological inputs overlap, so agreement
  between them is not independent validation.
- [Sleep Plus Stages](https://support.polar.com/en/sleep-plus-stages-sleep-tracking)
  describes amount, solidity and regeneration components. Component scores are
  exported as points. Start-to-end duration includes wake intervals and is not
  substituted for net sleep time.
- The current [Polar BLE SDK](https://github.com/polarofficial/polar-ble-sdk) lists
  [Vantage V3 data capabilities](https://github.com/polarofficial/polar-ble-sdk/blob/master/documentation/products/PolarVantageV3andGritX2Pro.md),
  including PPI and activity data. However, the
  [watch streaming procedure](https://github.com/polarofficial/polar-ble-sdk/blob/master/documentation/UsingSDKWithWatches.md)
  requires device-side sharing and an exercise-wait screen; it is not a drop-in
  replacement for unattended cloud collection. Healine therefore retains
  AccessLink and does not change the watch pairing or install a phone collector.
- Polar's [PPI quality guidance](https://github.com/polarofficial/polar-ble-sdk/blob/master/documentation/PPIData.md)
  requires attention to movement blockers and supported skin-contact flags.
  Sparse bpm samples cannot reconstruct beat-to-beat HRV. Adding daytime PPI
  later requires an actual supported source and quality checks; it cannot be
  synthesized from the current heart-rate stream.

## Recovery and synchronization checks

[Cloudflare documents Quick Tunnels as development infrastructure](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/networks/connectors/cloudflare-tunnel/do-more-with-tunnels/trycloudflare/).
Use a fixed collector-only origin with a supervised process. This preserves the
authorization boundary while avoiding a new random address after a restart.

[Apps Script quotas](https://developers.google.com/apps-script/guides/services/quotas)
include a six-minute execution limit and a 9KB limit per property. Historical
work is split into dates, with completion recorded only after Calendar writes;
failure of one date does not acknowledge it or prevent other due dates from
being processed. Detailed comparison caches are split into daily properties.

[Google Calendar usage limits](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/guides/quota)
also include operational throttling for rapid writes to one calendar, separate
from published API quotas. A creation-limit response pauses the writer across
dates with a persisted retry deadline; cached replay observes the same pause.
The chosen initial 15-minute retry delay is an operating policy, not a promise
that Google's restriction will have cleared by then.

[CalendarEvent setters and tags](https://developers.google.com/apps-script/reference/calendar/calendar-event)
support updating the same managed entries while preserving notes.
[Calendar incremental sync](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/guides/sync)
is relevant when consuming external Calendar changes, but a Calendar sync token
does not detect late Polar data. The collector therefore reconciles a rolling
source-date range and a durable repair queue instead of treating a successful
Calendar fetch as proof that all wearable history has arrived.

## What verification establishes

Regression tests cover source-field parsing, zero versus missing values, recovery
rehydration behind a newer failed response, same-day/future exclusion, known
sleep exclusion, matching current and previous activity, reference freshness,
preserving notes, duplicate-free writes, retry checkpoints and credential-origin
rollback. Live readback exposed Calendar's truncation of long descriptions;
regression coverage now simulates that behavior and verifies complete JSON and
preserved notes under the writer's conservative size budget. Replay against a
private frozen history checks comparison coverage and
reference dates. Neither synthetic tests nor increased coverage establish
psychological stress detection accuracy.

Future accuracy evaluation needs independent subjective labels aligned to actual
measurement times and a chronological holdout. Do not select thresholds on that
holdout, validate one derived recovery score against its own components, or use
Calendar event titles as ground-truth stress labels.
